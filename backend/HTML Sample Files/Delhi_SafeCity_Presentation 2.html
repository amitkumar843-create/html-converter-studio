Single Sub-Vendor Engagement
RailTel engaged a single sub-contractor (M/s Presto) for fiber laying & connectivity across all 16 Districts — creating a critical single point of failure with insufficient manpower. Frequent fiber cuts occur due to non-availability of sufficient manpower, and the majority of the fiber is overhead, increasing exposure.
Operations & Monitoring Gaps
Frequent link downtimes at multiple sites, well below contracted SLA. The Centralised NMS mandated under the RFP remains undeployed — leaving C-DAC without complete network visibility. JB restoration exceeds 2 days; complaint resolution delayed across multiple connected nodes.

Section 07 · Cross-Cutting Bottlenecks
Bottlenecks Under Delhi Safe City Project
Critical deficiencies in project governance, system design, operations monitoring and solution readiness · issue counts per category

PM & Planning Failures

3
  • Inability to forecast realistic completion timelines, resulting in project delay of over three years.
  • Failed to meet even downward-revised commitments accepted by Delhi Police to facilitate progress.
  • Continues to struggle in provisioning and stabilizing newly committed camera counts despite second revision.

Structural & Architectural Gaps

4
  • Flawed bandwidth architecture due to complete dependence on RailTel without resilient contingency framework.
  • Delhi Police forced to deal with sub-vendors to maintain momentum, creating accountability gaps.
  • Lack of unified end-to-end software; critical systems delivered in fragmented silos.
  • Software and SOPs require urgent upgrade and synchronization with Safe City national security objectives.

Operational Negligence & SLA

3
  • Absence of dedicated escalation and monitoring ownership for NMS tickets and Call Centre complaints.
  • Critical non-functional devices remain unattended for 10–20 days or more.
  • C-DAC team has shown inadequate proactiveness in follow-up with MSI and ISP for field issues.

System O&M Challenges

5
  • Core solution comprising FRS, ANPR and AIVA is non-functional since 13.04.2026 — unrestored after 2.5 months.
  • Real-time alert integration with PCR vans remains disjointed, breaking the response chain.
  • GIS maps do not track and display real-time locations of mobile vans for emergency deployment.
  • Live video feeds are poor quality, pixelated and analytically unusable.
  • Operational and field training is superficial; technical handholding by NEC remains inadequate.
