What moved this cycle
Network segmentation between the field VLANs and the analytics tier was completed at four of six sites, lifting nine controls out of Partially effective.
Centralised logging now covers the citizen portal and the ANPR tier, closing three long-standing observations.
Identity governance regressed: joiner-mover-leaver evidence could not be produced for 11 of 40 sampled privileged accounts.
Vendor assurance remains the weakest domain, with no independent attestation on file for two integration partners.
